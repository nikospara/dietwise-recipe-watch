1MyRecipeWatch — main workflow screen, three directions

Assumptions I made (say the word and I'll change any of them): the screen shows recipe name + ingredients only, never the preparation steps — there's a line linking back to the source page for those. One accepted swap per ingredient, unlimited rejections, and a rejection is styled as struck-through and gone from the queue (tap it to restore in this prototype). Suggestions carry a short "why" plus nutrition deltas, since a swap the user can't justify won't get accepted. Colours and type are the Organic system, warmed toward the DietWise orange mark. All three are live: accept, reject, undo.
